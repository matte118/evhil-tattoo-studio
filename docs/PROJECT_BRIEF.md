# Evhil Tattoo Studio — Project Brief

## 1. Project Overview

Evhil Tattoo Studio is a newly established tattoo and piercing studio based in Venegono Inferiore, Italy.

The website is a premium showcase website whose primary purpose is to:

1. establish Evhil as a recognizable contemporary tattoo brand;
2. showcase the quality of the studio's tattoo work;
3. introduce the artists and their individual portfolios;
4. communicate professionalism, competence, and customer care;
5. convert visitors primarily into WhatsApp conversations and secondarily into Instagram DMs.

Tattoo work is the primary business focus of the website.

Piercing is a secondary service and should be visible without competing with tattoo content.

## 3. Brand Positioning

Evhil should be positioned as a modern tattoo studio connected to the street roots of tattoo and piercing culture.

The desired positioning is a combination of:

- artistic studio / atelier;
- contemporary tattoo studio;
- street culture;
- modern youth culture;
- medium-high / premium perceived value.

The brand should feel:

- accessible;
- serious;
- raw;
- artistic;
- dark;
- visually rich;
- professional;
- creative;
- clean in execution.

The brand must not feel:

- dirty;
- careless;
- overly commercial;
- unprofessional;
- generic.

### Core Value Proposition

Evhil should communicate that:

- its staff is competent and up to date;
- tattoo projects are developed around the individual customer;
- designs should fit the person and body rather than feel generic;
- the quality and value of the tattoo work matters;
- the final result should feel carefully considered and exceptionally executed.

## 5. Customer Experience and Conversion

### Primary Conversion

Start a WhatsApp conversation.

### Secondary Conversion

Start an Instagram DM conversation.

### Current Customer Journey

Typical flow:

1. discovery through Instagram, word of mouth, Google, or the website;
2. customer initiates contact;
3. customer explains the idea;
4. customer shares visual references;
5. consultation / discussion;
6. quote;
7. deposit when applicable;
8. appointment.

The website does not need to replace this flow.

Its role is to increase trust, improve discovery, present the studio and artists, and make starting the conversation easier.

### Pricing

Do not display tattoo prices by default.

### Lead Qualification

Do not add a complex qualification form at launch.

### Booking

No integrated booking system at launch.

### Quote Requests

The "Request a Quote" experience should explain the process and then direct visitors to WhatsApp.

## 7. Information Architecture

Recommended primary navigation:

- Home
- Studio
- Artists
- Portfolio
- Piercing
- Request a Quote
- Contacts

The final navigation wording can be refined during design.

### Artist Detail Pages

Artist detail pages should exist but should not need to appear directly in the main navigation.

Expected structure:

`/artists/[slug]`

Each artist page may contain:

- artist introduction;
- specialization / tattoo styles;
- selected portfolio;
- available flash tattoos;
- Instagram or relevant social link;
- WhatsApp / consultation CTA where appropriate.

### Portfolio

A general portfolio page should exist.

The homepage should also surface selected work and link to the full portfolio.

Artist portfolios should remain accessible from the corresponding artist detail page.

### Events and Promotions

Events, flash days, or promotions may appear as a section on the homepage.

Do not create a dedicated events page at launch unless the amount or frequency of content later justifies it.

### Reviews

Reviews should primarily be presented within the homepage rather than as a standalone page.

### FAQ

FAQ is optional.

If included, prefer placing it inside the "Request a Quote" page instead of creating another top-level page.

### Location

Do not create a dedicated location page at launch.

Location information should be available in:

- Contacts;
- footer;
- structured business data;
- optional map / directions interaction.

### Aftercare

No dedicated aftercare page at launch.

### Blog

Do not create a blog at launch.

A blog should only be introduced if Evhil later adopts a real editorial or SEO content strategy.

### E-commerce

No e-commerce at launch.

Not in scope.

Not in scope.

## 13. SEO and Local SEO

A high-value target query is:

`tattoo studio Varese`

Additional local relevance may include:

- Venegono Inferiore;
- Varese;
- Como;
- Milano.

Do not create location pages solely to target keywords.

Local pages should only be created when they contain meaningful, distinct, useful content.

### SEO Baseline

The implementation should support:

- unique page titles;
- unique meta descriptions;
- canonical URLs;
- Open Graph metadata;
- sitemap;
- robots.txt;
- semantic headings;
- LocalBusiness structured data where appropriate;
- consistent NAP information;
- crawlable internal navigation;
- performant images;
- strong Core Web Vitals.

### Business Data

Business data should be centralized in one source of truth rather than manually duplicated across pages.

## 17. Content Management

The final content-management workflow is not yet decided.

Expected content may change relatively frequently, particularly:

- tattoo portfolio;
- artists;
- flash availability;
- events;
- promotional content.

The client may eventually need an easy editing interface.

However, the public website should not contain an administrative login area.

### Initial Architecture Requirement

Build content structures so that the site can initially use local structured content while remaining reasonably easy to migrate to a CMS later.

Do not install a CMS yet.

Sanity is a possible future option, not an approved dependency.

The content model should not unnecessarily couple presentation components to local file storage.

## 18. Technical Product Principles

The website is a premium showcase site, not a SaaS product.

Prioritize:

- visual quality;
- brand identity;
- maintainability;
- reusable components;
- static-first performance;
- strong mobile experience;
- SEO;
- accessibility;
- low operational complexity.

Avoid:

- unnecessary backend systems;
- authentication;
- databases;
- application-like architecture;
- premature CMS integration;
- unnecessary JavaScript;
- generic component-library aesthetics.

---

## 23. Definition of Product Success

The website succeeds if:

1. the experience immediately feels specific to Evhil rather than interchangeable with another tattoo studio;
2. tattoo work is presented as the primary proof of quality;
3. artists have enough space to develop their own identity and portfolio;
4. the studio feels professional, current, artistic, and rooted in street culture;
5. the site feels premium without becoming inaccessible or overly polished;
6. mobile visitors can understand the brand and reach WhatsApp easily;
7. the architecture remains simple enough to maintain and evolve;
8. replacing mock content with real production content does not require redesigning the application;
9. technical SEO, accessibility, performance, and responsive behavior are treated as baseline quality requirements.
