# Evhil Tattoo Studio

## Product Type

Evhil Tattoo Studio is a premium showcase website for a modern tattoo and piercing studio based in Venegono Inferiore, Italy.

This is a brand / portfolio / local-business website, not a SaaS product or web application.

Tattooing is the primary focus.
Piercing is secondary.

For complete business and product context, treat `docs/PROJECT_BRIEF.md` as the source of truth.

## Primary Goals

The website must:

1. establish Evhil as a distinctive contemporary tattoo brand;
2. showcase tattoo quality and artistic competence;
3. give individual artists meaningful visibility;
4. communicate professionalism, customer care, and premium perceived value;
5. drive qualified visitors primarily to WhatsApp and secondarily to Instagram DM.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- ages 18–40;
- mixed gender;
- tattoo-aware users;
- first-time and experienced tattoo customers;
- geographically relevant to Venegono Inferiore, Varese, Como, Milano, and surrounding areas.

The audience should be treated as visually literate and familiar with contemporary tattoo / street culture.

## Positioning

Evhil sits between:

- tattoo studio;
- artistic atelier;
- street culture;
- contemporary youth culture;
- medium-high / premium positioning.

The brand should feel:

- professional;
- artistic;
- street-rooted;
- contemporary;
- raw;
- dark;
- premium;
- accessible;
- confident;
- carefully executed.

It must not feel:

- amateur;
- dirty;
- careless;
- overly commercial;
- generic;
- corporate;
- like a stereotypical tattoo template.

## Core Value Proposition

Evhil's value is based on:

- competent and up-to-date artists;
- customer-specific tattoo design;
- work that fits the individual and the body;
- strong execution quality;
- care during the customer experience;
- the artistic and long-term value of the tattoo.

## Primary User Journey

Typical flow:

Discovery
→ explore brand and tattoo work
→ evaluate artists and trust
→ understand how the studio works
→ start a WhatsApp conversation
→ share idea and references
→ consultation
→ quote
→ deposit / appointment when applicable

The website should support this journey without replacing it with a complex booking flow.

## Primary Conversion

**WhatsApp conversation**

## Secondary Conversion

**Instagram DM**

Do not introduce a custom booking engine or complex lead form at launch.

## Main Content

The experience should prioritize:

1. tattoo work;
2. brand identity;
3. artists;
4. studio atmosphere;
5. reviews / trust;
6. quote process;
7. piercing;
8. events / promotions when relevant.

## Main Pages

- Home
- Studio
- Artists
- Artist detail pages
- Portfolio
- Piercing
- Request a Quote
- Contacts

Artist detail pages should not need to appear directly in the main navigation.

## Content Integrity

Never invent production facts.

Real artist names, biographies, reviews, awards, ratings, events, prices, certifications, opening hours, and portfolio claims must be verified before publication.

Mock content is allowed during development only when clearly treated as placeholder content.

AI-generated tattoo work must never be presented as real Evhil work.

## Content Language

User-facing website copy: **Italian**.

Technical implementation, component names, variables, data properties, and developer documentation: **English**.

## Voice

The website voice should be:

- professional;
- direct;
- artistic;
- street-aware;
- confident.

Avoid inflated marketing claims and generic tattoo clichés.

## Anti-References

Evhil should not resemble:

- a SaaS landing page;
- a generic dark-mode template;
- a corporate service website;
- a gothic tattoo cliché;
- a cyberpunk theme;
- an ecommerce catalog;
- a component-library demo.

## Product Success

The website succeeds when visitors:

- immediately perceive Evhil as a specific brand;
- see convincing tattoo work early;
- understand the role and identity of individual artists;
- trust the studio;
- feel both street credibility and professional quality;
- can reach WhatsApp easily;
- experience a strong, intentional mobile presentation.
