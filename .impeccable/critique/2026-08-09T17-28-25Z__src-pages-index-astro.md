## Overall Impression

The page opens with a convincing campaign-quality peak and ends with a strong local/contact moment. The biggest weakness is the trust-building middle: artists, events and reviews are necessarily temporary, so production-sized placeholder sections create a prolonged emotional valley.

## What's Working

- The hero is unmistakably EVHIL: asymmetric, image-led, graffiti-visible and conversion-ready.
- The architecture follows a coherent customer journey from identity and work through process and contact.
- Content integrity and accessibility are disciplined: references are labelled, unavailable items are noninteractive, focus is visible and reduced motion is supported.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Placeholder-only sections create a proof vacuum.** Artists, events and reviews carry full production weight while providing no verified social proof. **Fix:** compress these states until real content exists while retaining the required architecture. Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`.
- **[P1] Reference disclosure becomes visual noise.** Multiple overlays, captions and explanatory statements repeat the same caveat. **Fix:** use one consistent image disclosure plus one concise section-level explanation. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P2] Temporary font fallbacks limit brand credibility.** The largest type moments depend on generic local metrics. **Fix:** replace them with approved licensed files or protected EVHIL lettering when supplied, then retune line breaks. Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`.
- **[P2] Conversion continuity weakens on a long mobile page.** The primary quote route is not continuously accessible after the hero. **Fix:** maintain a compact persistent header/WhatsApp action and protect anchor offsets. Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`.

## Minor Observations

- “Reference / preview” should remain consistently Italian.
- The final typography must be revalidated once approved font assets exist.
- “Deposito” should remain only while that studio process is explicitly approved for publication.
